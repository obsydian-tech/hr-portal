# Swilco Systems — Meeting Guide
### Obsydian Technologies · Melbourne · 23 May 2026

---

## 1. Land Obsydian First (Say This Early)
> *"I'm the technical director at Obsydian Technologies — a software company I lead. When you engage
> with me, you're engaging Obsydian. We embed as your technical leadership. You get me as your CTO,
> and behind me you get the full development capability of the company. Once scope is agreed, we work
> in milestones — implementation fee upfront, payments tied to delivery."*

---

## 2. The Golden Rule for This Meeting
**The best platforms are not built by technology. They are built by deeply understanding the problem first.**
Every dashboard, AI feature, mobile app, and buzzword comes *after* you know exactly whose life is
broken and why. Your job today is to find that — not to sell features.

---

## 3. The Discovery Questions (Do Not Skip These)

| # | Ask This |
|---|----------|
| 1 | **"Who is kept up at night in your organisation — and what is keeping them up?"** |
| 2 | **"What is the one thing that wastes the most time or costs the most money right now?"** |
| 3 | **"When something goes wrong — missed shift, compliance breach, no-show — what does fixing it look like today?"** |
| 4 | **"Who are the real end users? The coordinator? The support worker in the field? The family?"** |
| 5 | **"What does success look like in 6 months if this works?"** |

> Listen more than you speak. Reflect back what you hear before moving forward.

---

## 4. Redirect the Buzzword Temptation
If the conversation drifts to AI, dashboards, or features — bring it back:
> *"All of that is achievable. But platforms that fail start with the tech. The ones that succeed
> start with a problem so painful that people will change their behaviour to fix it.
> What is that problem for you?"*

---

## 5. The Obsydian Difference (Say This Once)
> *"Most development studios build what you ask for. We help you figure out what to ask for.
> That's the difference between a platform that gets used and one that gets shelved."*

---

## 6. Phase 1 — The Only Scope Conversation Today
If scope comes up, anchor to **three pillars only:**

- **Shift & workforce management** — rostering, availability, GPS clock-in/out
- **AI voice-to-note** — care workers record verbally, AI produces compliance-ready shift notes
- **Provider admin dashboard** — workforce overview, compliance tracking, document expiry

Everything else — payroll, participant portals, family comms, predictive AI — is **Phase 2 and beyond.**

---

## 7. The Engagement Model Question
Let them answer — do not lead:
> *"Do you see this as a one-time build, or a longer-term relationship where you have technical
> leadership growing the platform with you?"*

Their answer shapes everything commercially. **Do not quote numbers today.**

---

## 8. How to Close
> *"We have enough from today to put a proper scope and proposal together. Next step is a 2-hour
> scoping session — after that, a written proposal with scope, milestones, and costs within
> 5 business days. Can we lock that in before we leave today?"*

---

> **Three rules: Never quote a number today. Never agree Phase 1 includes Phase 2.
> Always close with a specific next step.**

---

## 9. Pre-Meeting Texts
*Send these tonight — casual, warm, gets you intel.*

**Text 1 — Set the tone and prime them**
> "Hey [name], looking forward to tomorrow. Just wanted to make sure we make the most of the time — I've been doing some thinking on your platform and I want to make sure we go deep on the right things. Quick question before we meet: what's the one operational problem that costs you the most time or money right now in the business?"

**Text 2 — Size the operation**
> "Also — roughly how many support workers or staff are you currently managing? Just want to calibrate the right solution for where you're at today versus where you're heading."

**Text 3 — Signal the meeting structure**
> "My plan for tomorrow is to spend the first part really understanding your operation before we talk solutions — the best platforms I've built started that way. Then we can talk about what a first phase looks like and how we'd work together. Does that work for you?"
